\begin{abstract}
    物理知识嵌入的复杂场景生成与仿真旨在将材料本构关系、力学属性及物理定律系统性地融入三维视觉表征，使虚拟场景不仅具备静态几何外观，更能在外部激励下遵循真实物理规律产生形变与动态交互，已成为机器人仿真、数字孪生与具身智能等领域的重要研究方向。面向复杂环境下的自主机器人仿真训练与部署前评估需求，构建高保真虚拟仿真场景是降低真实环境测试成本与风险的关键途径。传统物理仿真环境依赖繁琐的人工建模，在场景生成效率、物理参数映射细粒度以及视觉-物理时空一致性上存在显著不足。以三维高斯溅射（3DGS）为代表的视觉表征方法虽能快速恢复场景外观，却缺乏质量、弹性、密度等力学本构属性，无法支撑物理碰撞与动态交互。针对上述问题，本文设计并实现了一种物理知识嵌入的复杂场景生成与仿真系统，贯通了从文本/图像输入到可仿真虚拟场景的端到端技术链路，本文的主要工作与创新成果如下：

    （1）针对复杂场景中从视觉表观到物理属性的自动化映射问题，构建了面向物理仿真的三维场景生成与材质初始化管线。以前馈式重建方法从文本或图像生成三维高斯场景雏形；以通用分割模型与视觉语言大模型对场景各区域进行逐区域材质推理，通过经验映射库将语义标签转化为杨氏模量、泊松比与密度的初始物理参数区间，为后续物理参数优化提供约束先验。

    （2）针对纯视觉三维表征缺乏力学语义、且大规模静态背景在仿真中易引发计算发散的问题，提出了基于三维高斯的联合语义与物理材质感知的特征蒸馏与前景分离框架。将跨模态视觉-语言语义特征、自监督视觉结构特征以及视觉语言大模型推理的材质分类标签，通过可微 $\alpha$-blending 联合蒸馏至三维高斯原语，使每个高斯粒子同时具备视觉语义感知与物理材质归属的双重属性；在此基础上通过前景分割管线将可交互前景主体与静态背景解耦并冻结，确保仅前景区域进入后续物理求解器。

    （3）针对经验物理参数与真实力学响应存在偏差、且缺乏真值物理标注的问题，提出了多材质物质点法（MPM）连续体仿真与视频扩散先验引导的物理参数优化方法。为不同材质区域独立分配弹性、粘弹性与弹塑性等本构模型，以视频扩散模型为隐式物理先验，通过分数蒸馏采样（SDS）将仿真帧与预期运动偏差转化为杨氏模量的优化梯度，经可微物理管线反传至各物质点。实验表明，视频扩散先验优化后的仿真视频在视频质量评测基准上取得最优美学质量与成像质量，验证了视觉语义与物理参数联合建模的有效性。

    \keywordslist{复杂场景生成, 物理仿真, 特征蒸馏, 视觉先验优化}
\end{abstract}

% 英文摘要
\begin{engabstract}
    The generation and simulation of complex scenes embedded in physical knowledge aims to systematically integrate material constitutive relations, mechanical properties and physical laws into three-dimensional visual representation, so that the virtual scene not only has a static geometric appearance, but also can follow the real physical laws to generate deformation and dynamic interaction under external excitation. It has become an important research direction in the fields of robot simulation, digital twin and embodied intelligence. Facing the requirements of simulation training and pre-deployment evaluation of autonomous robots in complex environments, constructing high-fidelity virtual simulation scenarios is a key way to reduce the cost and risk of real environment testing. The traditional physical simulation environment relies on tedious manual modeling, which has significant deficiencies in scene generation efficiency, fine-grained physical parameter mapping, and visual-physical spatio-temporal consistency. Although the visual representation method represented by three-dimensional Gaussian sputtering ( 3DGS ) can quickly restore the appearance of the scene, it lacks mechanical constitutive properties such as mass, elasticity, and density, and cannot support physical collision and dynamic interaction. In view of the above problems, this paper designs and implements a complex scene generation and simulation system with physical knowledge embedding, which runs through the end-to-end technical link from text / image input to simulated virtual scene. The main work and innovations of this paper are as follows : 
 
    ( 1 ) Aiming at the problem of automatic mapping from visual appearance to physical attributes in complex scenes, a three-dimensional scene generation and material initialization pipeline for physical simulation is constructed. Generate a three-dimensional Gaussian scene prototype from text or image by feedforward reconstruction method ; the general segmentation model and the visual language model are used to perform region-by-region material reasoning on each region of the scene. The semantic label is transformed into the initial physical parameter interval of Young 's modulus, Poisson 's ratio and density through the experience mapping library, which provides constraint priors for subsequent physical parameter optimization. 

    ( 2 ) Aiming at the problem that pure visual three-dimensional representation lacks mechanical semantics and large-scale static background is easy to cause computational divergence in simulation, a feature distillation and foreground separation framework based on three-dimensional Gaussian joint semantics and physical material perception is proposed. The material classification labels of cross-modal visual-verbal semantic features, self-supervised visual structural features and visual language big model reasoning are jointly distilled into three-dimensional Gaussian primitives through differentiable $\alpha$-blending, so that each Gaussian particle has the dual attributes of visual semantic perception and physical material attribution. On this basis, the interactive foreground subject and the static background are decoupled and frozen through the foreground segmentation pipeline to ensure that only the foreground area enters the subsequent physical solver.

    ( 3 ) Aiming at the problem that the empirical physical parameters deviate from the real mechanical response and lack of physical annotation of the true value, a multi-material material point method ( MPM ) continuum simulation and video diffusion prior guided physical parameter optimization method is proposed. The constitutive models of elasticity, viscoelasticity and elastoplasticity are independently distributed for different material regions. The video diffusion model is used as the implicit physical prior. The simulation frame and the expected motion deviation are transformed into the optimal gradient of Young 's modulus by fractional distillation sampling ( SDS ), which is transmitted back to each material point through the differential physical pipeline. Experiments show that the simulation video optimized by video diffusion prior achieves the best aesthetic quality and imaging quality on the video quality evaluation benchmark, which verifies the effectiveness of joint modeling of visual semantics and physical parameters.

    \engkeywordslist { complex scene generation, physical simulation, feature distillation, visual prior optimization }
\end{engabstract}
