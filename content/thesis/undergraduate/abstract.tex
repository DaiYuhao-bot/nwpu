% 中文摘要
\begin{abstract}
    面向复杂场景下的物理仿真与动态生成需求，本文设计并实现了一种物理知识嵌入的复杂场景生成与仿真系统。该系统以文本或参考图像为输入，通过前馈式生成管线构建三维高斯场景表示；采用 SAM 与深度聚类分层实现二维场景的空间解耦，并引入视觉语言模型对分割区域进行材质识别与标注。

    系统结合材质物理属性先验与 VLM 推断结果，为场景中不同区域分配仿真本构模型及初始物理参数范围。在物理属性映射阶段，通过特征蒸馏将视觉蒸馏特征和 VLM 推理的材质语义标签同步映射至高斯特征空间，使各高斯粒子兼具视觉蒸馏与物理材质双重表征。

    在物理参数优化阶段，系统通过文本驱动的前景分割机制冻结静态背景以保留前景主体。前景区域输入物理参数优化模块，在 Stable Video Diffusion（SVD）视频先验引导下，梯度优化各高斯点的杨氏模量等物理参数，使基于 MPM 的仿真场景收敛至 SVD 视频先验分布。

    本文系统集成了场景生成、材质理解与映射、物理仿真三个环节，实现了从文本/图像输入到三维生成、物理属性赋予、仿真优化的完整链路。相较于依赖手工设定物理参数的传统方案，该系统为不同材质区域提供合理的物理先验并细化区域物理属性。实验表明，该流程在帽子、花瓶、雕塑等案例上均成功完成完整链路，为复杂场景仿真与物理知识嵌入研究提供了可复现的实现基础。
    \keywordslist{复杂场景生成, 物理仿真, 材质蒸馏, 语义分割}
\end{abstract}

% 英文摘要
\begin{engabstract}
    Aiming at the requirements of physical simulation and dynamic generation in complex scenes, this paper designs and implements a system of complex scene generation and simulation with physical knowledge embedding. The system takes text or reference image as input, and constructs a three-dimensional Gaussian scene representation by feedforward generation pipeline. Then, SAM and deep clustering are used to segment the 2D scene hierarchically, and the visual language model is introduced to identify and label the material of the segmented area. 
 
    Combining the material physical property prior and the VLM inference results, the simulation constitutive model and the initial physical property range are assigned to different regions in the scene, and the constraint prior is provided for the subsequent physical property optimization. In the stage of physical attribute mapping, the system maps the distilled visual features and the material semantic labels predicted by VLM inference to the Gaussian feature space synchronously based on the feature distillation method, so that each Gaussian particle has both distilled visual and physical material double representation. 
 
    In the stage of physical attribute optimization, the system freezes the static background through the text-driven foreground segmentation mechanism, and selects the retained foreground subject. The foreground region is input into the physical attribute optimization module, and the physical parameters such as Young 's modulus of each Gaussian point are optimized by gradient under the guidance of Stable Video Diffusion ( SVD ) video prior, so that the MPM-based simulation scene is visually closer to the video prior generated by SVD.

    In this paper, the system integrates three links : scene generation, material understanding and mapping, and physical simulation, and realizes a complete link from two-dimensional input to three-dimensional generation, to physical attribute assignment, and then to simulation optimization. Compared with the traditional scheme, this method can stably provide reasonable physical priors for different material areas, and finely optimize the physical properties of the area, which improves the availability of complex scene simulation. Experiments show that the process has successfully completed the complete process in hats, vases, sculptures and other cases, which provides a reproducible implementation basis for complex scene simulation and physical knowledge embedding research.
    \engkeywordslist { complex scene generation, physical simulation, material distillation, semantic segmentation }
\end{engabstract}
