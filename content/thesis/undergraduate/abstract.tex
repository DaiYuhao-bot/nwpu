\begin{abstract}
    物理知识嵌入的复杂场景生成与仿真旨在将材料本构关系、力学属性及物理定律系统性地融入三维视觉表征。这一技术使虚拟场景在具备静态几何外观的同时，能够在外部激励下遵循真实物理规律产生形变与动态交互。该方向已成为机器人仿真、数字孪生与具身智能等领域的重要研究课题。面向复杂环境下的自主机器人仿真训练与部署前评估需求，构建高保真虚拟仿真场景是降低真实环境测试成本与风险的关键途径。传统物理仿真环境依赖繁重的人工建模，在场景生成效率、物理参数映射的细粒度以及视觉-物理时空一致性上存在显著不足。以三维高斯溅射（3DGS）为代表的视觉表征方法虽能快速恢复场景外观，却缺乏质量、弹性、密度等力学本构属性，无法支撑物理碰撞与动态交互。针对上述问题，本文设计并实现了一种物理知识嵌入的复杂场景生成与仿真系统。该系统贯通了从文本/图像输入到可仿真虚拟场景的端到端技术链路。本文的主要工作与创新成果如下：

    （1）针对复杂场景中从视觉表征到物理属性的自动化映射问题，构建了面向物理仿真的三维场景生成与材质初始化管线。以前馈式重建方法从文本或图像生成三维高斯场景雏形；以通用分割模型与视觉语言大模型对场景各区域进行材质推理。通过材质经验库将材质标签转化为杨氏模量、泊松比与密度的初始物理参数区间，为后续物理参数优化提供约束先验。

    （2）针对纯视觉三维表征缺乏力学属性信息、且大规模静态背景在仿真中易引发计算发散的问题，提出了基于三维高斯的视觉语义与物理材质联合感知的特征蒸馏与前景背景解耦框架。将跨模态视觉-语言语义特征、自监督视觉结构特征以及视觉语言大模型推理的材质标签，通过可微 $\alpha$-blending 联合蒸馏至三维高斯粒子，使每个高斯粒子同时具备视觉语义感知与物理材质归属的双重属性。在此基础上，通过前景分割管线将可交互前景主体与静态背景解耦并冻结，确保仅前景区域进入后续物理求解器。

    （3）针对经验物理参数与真实力学响应存在偏差、且缺乏真值物理标注的问题，提出了多材质物质点法（MPM）连续体仿真与视频扩散先验引导的物理参数优化方法。为不同材质区域独立分配弹性、粘弹性与弹塑性等本构模型。以视频扩散模型为隐式物理先验，通过分数蒸馏采样（SDS）将仿真帧与预期运动之间的偏差转化为杨氏模量的优化梯度，经可微物理管线反传至各物质点。实验表明，视频扩散先验优化后的仿真视频在视频质量评测基准上取得最优美学质量与成像质量，验证了视觉语义与物理参数联合建模的有效性。

    \keywordslist{复杂场景生成, 物理仿真, 特征蒸馏, 视觉先验优化}
\end{abstract}

% 英文摘要
\begin{engabstract}
    Physical-knowledge-embedded complex scene generation and simulation aims to integrate material constitutive models, mechanical properties, and physical laws into three-dimensional visual representations. This enables virtual scenes to preserve static geometry and appearance while producing physically plausible deformation and dynamic interaction under external forces. It has become an important research topic in robot simulation, digital twins, and embodied intelligence. For simulation training and pre-deployment evaluation of autonomous robots in complex environments, high-fidelity virtual scenes are essential for reducing the cost and risk of real-world testing. However, conventional physical simulation environments rely heavily on manual modeling and remain limited in scene generation efficiency, fine-grained physical-parameter assignment, and visual-physical spatiotemporal consistency. Although visual representations such as three-dimensional Gaussian splatting (3DGS) can efficiently reconstruct scene appearance, they lack mechanical properties such as mass, elasticity, and density, and therefore cannot directly support physical collision and dynamic interaction. To address these limitations, this thesis designs and implements a physical-knowledge-embedded complex scene generation and simulation system that connects text/image input to simulatable virtual scenes in an end-to-end pipeline. The main contributions are as follows:

    (1) To automate the mapping from visual representations to physical properties in complex scenes, a 3D scene generation and material initialization pipeline for physical simulation is constructed. A feed-forward reconstruction method is used to generate an initial 3D Gaussian scene from text or image input. A general segmentation model and a vision-language model are then used to infer the material of each scene region. Through an empirical material library, material labels are mapped to initial ranges of Young's modulus, Poisson's ratio, and density, providing constrained priors for subsequent physical-parameter optimization.

    (2) To address the lack of mechanical-property information in purely visual 3D representations and the numerical instability caused by large-scale static backgrounds, a 3D-Gaussian-based feature distillation and foreground-background decoupling framework is proposed for joint visual-semantic and physical-material perception. Cross-modal vision-language semantic features, self-supervised visual structural features, and material labels predicted by the vision-language model are jointly distilled into 3D Gaussian particles through differentiable $\alpha$-blending. As a result, each Gaussian particle is equipped with both visual-semantic perception and physical material attribution. A foreground segmentation pipeline is further used to decouple and freeze the interactive foreground from the static background, ensuring that only the foreground region enters the physical solver.

    (3) To reduce the discrepancy between empirical physical parameters and real mechanical responses under the absence of ground-truth physical annotations, a multi-material Material Point Method (MPM) simulation and video-diffusion-prior-guided physical-parameter optimization method is proposed. Elastic, viscoelastic, and elastoplastic constitutive models are assigned independently to different material regions. A video diffusion model serves as an implicit physical prior, and Score Distillation Sampling (SDS) converts the deviation between simulated frames and expected motion into gradients for optimizing Young's modulus. These gradients are back-propagated to material points through a differentiable physical pipeline. Experiments show that videos optimized with the video diffusion prior achieve the best aesthetic quality and imaging quality on video-quality benchmarks, demonstrating the effectiveness of joint visual-semantic and physical-parameter modeling.

    \engkeywordslist{complex scene generation, physical simulation, feature distillation, visual prior optimization}
\end{engabstract}
